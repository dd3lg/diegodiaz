Fundamentals of Design

Exploring balance, contrast, alignment, and visual hierarchy through structured compositions.

Overview

In this project, I explored the core principles of graphic design, including balance, contrast, alignment, and proximity. The goal was to create visually effective layouts that clearly communicate ideas.

Process

I began by experimenting with different compositions and grid structures. Through critique and revision, I refined my work to improve clarity and visual hierarchy.

Reflection

This project helped me understand how small design decisions impact the overall effectiveness of a layout. I learned how to organize elements in a way that guides the viewer’s attention.
